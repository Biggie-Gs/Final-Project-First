About Me

Hi, my name is Harrison. I've only been playing Magic the Gathering for a few years but I've loved playing every second of it. I started playing Magic the Gathering in the middle of highschool when I became friends with another guy who loved Magic the Gathering. It all started when he had brought a deck to play commander with another friend in our friend group. As I stared at the cards, I didn't understand them or think I'd care about them down the line. Fortunatly, my friend showed me how to play and eventually, let me borrow one of his decks to play with in a commander game against him. I lost, obviously, but it was great fun and I've been playing ever since! My favorite colors in Magic is Orzhov/White and Black. I love aristocrats style of play because it creates a bunch of tokens that can either be thrown as cannon fodder or be used as a resource. I do like to play taxes like but I know everybody at the table immediatly becomes your enemy once you profit off of them so I usually stick to just aristocrats.

First deck

Funnily enough, the first deck I ever made was due to spite and because I thought it'd be funny. The gist of the deck is to make my creatures indestructable and then just destroy all creatures constantly so I can continue full swinging at my opponents. Its not a very good deck but its a gimmick deck and brought me lots of joy whenever it was working. Basically, Odric(the commander) gives keywords to all my creatures at the begining of combat for each creature that I have with that keyword, so you give any creature indestructable. Swing with a few creatures and then in your second main phase you play any of the creature boardwipes so that all your oppponents creatures will get destroyed but none of your own.
