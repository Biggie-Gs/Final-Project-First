Magic the Gathering is a unique TCG in the sense that compared to the other big TCG's its the only one that requires resources to even play the game. In yugioh you set down a creature and it does its stuff and in pokemon you set down your pokemon and use energies to allow it to attack.

In Magic the Gathering,

The only card you're allowed to play from your hand without paying anything is your lands which generate the mana you need to actually play your other cards.

Lands generate Mana

Magic the Gathering has 5 colors and a colorless. These colors are White, Blue, Red, Green, and Black. These colors corrospond with a basic land, these are Plains for White, Islands for Blue, Mountains for Red, Forests for Green, and Swamps for Black. Colorless has a basic land called Wastes.

Use Mana to cast Spells

Each of these lands will be tapped a.k.a used up for a color of its type. Using these we can cast spells which are anything that isn't a land. Spells include Creatures, Artifcats, Enchantments, Plainswalkers, Sorceries, and Instants. Each of these spells have a cost in the top right of their card.

The Different types of Spells

  • Creatures

    Creatures are a type of permenant. You can tell something is a creature by looking at the bottem left of the card where you'll find a #/# where the first # is its power and the second # is toughness. Power determines the damage that the creature will do either during combat. Toughness is the amount of damage that the creature can take before it is destroyed. Most creatures will have a ability below the art that specifies something to do on a condition. This can be a triggered ability as in when something happens then the ability triggers, this can be as simple as when it enters the battlefield or something more complex like when another ability triggers. Activated abilites are abilites that you can activate for a cost at any response time unless otherwise specified. On the turn your creature comes into play it has something called summoning sickness. Creatures with summoning sickness cannot tap for activated abilities or be declared as an attacker until your next turn.

  • Artifacts

    Artifacts are another permenant type, most of the time they will have an activated ability with them, these can range from gaining mana, drawing cards, or even creating/destroying creatures. Most of the time these artifacts will have a generic mana cost only as in the 1,2,3,4,5,6.

  • Enchantments

    Enchantments are very much like artifacts, with the main difference being that enchantments usually have either static abilities like giving your creatures extra toughness or triggered abilites like giving you mana when a creature enters. The other difference with Enchantments are that they are usually are colored.

  • Planeswalkers

    Planeswalkers are a very unique permenant as one of only a few things players can attack. Planeswalkers will have a loyalty counter at the bottem right of their card indicating its health. Once it's health gets to 0, it will be destroyed. They will usually have a few activated abilites, where only one can be activated during your turns. They will have a cost assosiated with them either adding loyalty, removing loyalty, or being 0. Planeswalkers have whats usually called ultimates as the very bottem activated ability. They're usually cost more loyalty than the Planeswalker has loyalty when it enters the battlefield. Planeswalkers have whats called emblems. Emblems are not permenants but are abilites that are tied to you. The only way to remove an Emblem is to restart the game.

  • Sorcery

    Sorceries are not permenants. Once you cast them and pay the mana, you get the effect and they go immediatly to graveyard. Sorceries can only be cast during your main phases. Sorceries can be of any color too!

  • Instants

    Instants are like sorceries but they can be cast in response to anything or cast at the same time of a sorcery. Anything that has a Flash keyword underneath the art acts like an instant!

General turns

At the very start you are in your uptap phase which goes
Untap- Untap all your permenants
Upkeep- Anything that says upkeep triggers now
Draw - Draw your card for turn

After that you will go into your first main phase. In your main phase you are allowed to cast any cards in your hand and place lands onto the battlefield. to cast a spell you will, place down your card face up and look at the top right corner for its cost, you will tap lands and other pemenants for colors equal to its specific color cost and any other mix of mana for its generic cost. After you are down placing your one land for the turn and casting any spells you want, you will move into the combat phase.

During combat there are a few phases which are.
Start of combat- Combat phase starts
Declare attackers- tap any of your creatures without summoning sickness to declare them as an attacker at any players, planeswalkers, etc.
Declare defenders - The player who you've declared attacks against wether it be the player or the controller of the permenant you are attacking, is allowed to declare any creatures as blockers against your cretures.
First strike phase- Any creatures with the keyword double strike or first strike will deal damage in this phase
Damage phase - normal creatures will deal damage in this phase
End of combat - All creatures with damage equal to or more than their toughness will be sent to graveyard, players will change their life totals to reflect damage that went through, and planeswalkers who were attacked will remove loyalty counters equal to the damage and be destroyed if their loyalty is 0.

After Combat, you will get another main phase to do anything else with your turn.
After your second main phase you will go to end of turn, where end of turn effects happen and if you have more than 7 cards in hand you will discard down to handsize.

Now that you know what the cards are and what to do during your turn, there are a few things to note for when you're playing

  • Battlefield - Its where all permenants reside and basically where everything is unless stated as another zone
  • Library - The cards in your deck in which you will draw from
  • Graveyard - A zone you will designate, its where you will discard cards from hand into and put permenants that are destroyed into.
  • Exile - A seperate zone you will designate, it is like the graveyard but harder to interact with
  • Keywords - Some creatures and cards with have a 1-2 words under the art, these are abilities that show up on many cards. These include words like Flash, Haste, First Strike, Vigilance, etc.
  • Mill - Some cards will tell you to mill cards, this means to put cards from the top of your library into the graveyard equal to the mill amount
  • Supertype - special designation for cards, basically cards with special rules which include Basic, Legendary, World, and Snow
  • Card type - What the card is like, this is the Creature, Planeswalker, Instant, Land, Sorceries, etc.
  • Sub type - Whenever a card type has something next to it after an -, it indicates a special designation within that card type like Elf in the creature card pictured at the top
  • board wipe - Removes all of a certain thing. Basically wiping the board clean of creatures, lands, artifacts, etc.
  • Life - amount of damage you can take before you lose the game
