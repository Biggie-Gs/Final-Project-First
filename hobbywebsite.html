Magic the Gathering

Magic the Gathering is a tabletop card game otherwise known as a TCG. The basic permise of a TCG is there are packs of specific cards that you buy and open. With said cards you would make yourself a deck to play a specific mode of Magic the Gathering.

New Player About Different Game Types Deck Themes

Magic the Gathering was created back in 1993 at gencon with the limited edition Alpha set and ever since Magic the Gathering has been a great part of many peoples lives. It brings friends together and has many different ways to play. This allows people to use the 32 years of cards to play the way they want to. If they like quick games they may play a lot of cards early on, if they want it slow and methodical then they may play with cards the stop their opponent from casting their biggest spells, and some people just want to get the biggest number on their cards as possible. These are all ways people have fun while deck building and playing Magic the Gathering!
