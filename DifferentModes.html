Standard

Standard is what you'll find is the most general version of magic and most turnaments are played in the Standard format It's a 1v1 mode where everyone gets 20 life and 60 card decks

Standard info

Commander

Commander is what many causal players love to play. It's a free for all but usually played between 3-6 players. Everyone creates a 100 card deck based on a legendary creature and only uses cards with said creatures colors for the cards. Every player gets 40 life and many games devolve into politics and alliances between certain players at the table. Its great with friends!

Commander info

Modern

Modern is like Standard in that it is a 1v1, 60 card, 20 life game mode. The key difference being the banlist! Modern is played with cards all the way back to eight edition and foward while Standard has a rotating banlist.

Modern info

Legacy

Legacy is like Standard and Modern where its a 1v1, 60 card, 20 life game mode but it goes back to any set from Magic's past. Most of the meta decks in legacy are costly though so be warned!
